THE PUBLIC STABILITY METRIC

Mean weekly Gini + 88 × min(0, slope) − 0.5 × residual standard deviation

How it is calculated

Fit a least-squares line to the 24 Gini values using week positions 0–23. Calculate population standard deviation around that fitted line. A negative slope receives a penalty; a positive slope receives no bonus. These public metric coefficients are fixed, not tunable controls. The sample retains a mean Gini of 0.6000 as the sliders change.
